PE 2 — Basic Basketball for Women

Journal Log

A running record of my basketball experiences this midyear — what we drilled, what clicked, what didn't, and what I want to work on next.

YB
Christina Ysabelle Budikey · PE 2 BS-W
Journal 3
July 24, 2026

For the past week of playing basketball, I have realized that I have forgotten most of what I learned a year ago. While I still remember how to dribble and shoot, I have a lot more to learn when it comes to team tactics and defense. Even though I need more training on these skills, being with my classmates motivates me to do my best, despite the hot and humid environment. Whenever the ball goes through the hoop, I feel proud because I used to tell myself that I was horrible at shooting.

I really want to master my dribbling; surprisingly, my right hand has more control over the ball than my left, even though I am left-handed. I also want to improve my shooting, especially in a game setting, as I have a tendency to panic and rush my shot with less-than-ideal form. Having these goals motivates me to work harder because I truly enjoy playing the sport.

Journal 2
July 10, 2026

Practicing basketball individually allowed me to focus heavily on my personal mechanics, primarily shooting and dribbling. However, transitioning to 3x3 games made me realize how many aspects of the sport I have yet to master, communication in particular. I have learned the value of strategizing as a team and reading the game through our collective body language. Furthermore, being part of a team has shown me how our diverse skill sets complement one another. I realized that my teammates often excel where I struggle, allowing us to balance out our individual weaknesses and bring out each other's strengths.

Our team finds its strongest synergy when we are on defense. While we still occasionally struggle under pressure when we possess the ball (sometimes letting panic disrupt our passing game), I have noticed a significant improvement in how we handle high-stress moments lately. These minor setbacks can easily be resolved with more practice and in-game experience.

Because our team has grown incredibly close, teamwork and cooperation come naturally to us; everyone gives their absolute best effort to execute the specific roles that align with our playing styles. On a personal level, defense is my strongest asset. My classmates often mention how difficult it is to break past my defense or to guard me when I am on the move. While my teammates have pointed out that I excel as the "drive of the game" — acting as the primary ball-handler in the middle to control the pace — I honestly feel I still have room to grow in that role. Moving forward, I want to actively hone my skills by focusing on my blocking and stealing to become a more well-rounded player.
